# Screen guide

The screenshots come from the 18Birdies app. Classify each image with one of these `screen_type` values.

- **`round_summary`** (Round Summary). A header with the date (M/D/YY), weather, the course name with an optional "(subname)", and a line like `Course Par: 71  Tee: White 5516 yds  Duration: 4h 49m`. A card shows `To Par +22` and `Gross/Net 93/78`: the first number of Gross/Net is `displayed.gross`. Below it is a condensed score grid: row 1 is holes 1-9 with the front-nine total at the far right (`displayed.front`); row 2 is holes 10-18 with the back-nine total (`displayed.back`). Hole numbers are small, above each score, and each score is drawn inside its symbol. Par, putts, fairways and GIR per hole are **not** shown here. Newer versions add a "Basic Stats" card (fairway and GIR percentage donuts and total Putts): copy total putts to `displayed.total_putts` only if it is printed as a count.
- **`scorecard_scores`** (Full Scorecard, Scores view; reached from "View Full Scorecard"). Holes are columns, followed by Out / In / Tot. Rows: Hole, Par, Handicap (= stroke index, `si`), sometimes yardage for the tee, then each player's Gross and Net rows. "4/5" in a Par or Handicap cell is a men's/women's pair. In portrait only about six holes fit, so the card is split across horizontally swiped screenshots; landscape shows more holes per image. Read the requested player's Gross row for `strokes`, and the Out / In / Tot cells of that row for `displayed.front` / `back` / `gross`.
- **`scorecard_stats`** (Full Scorecard, Stats view; the "Scores / Stats" toggle). One player's per-hole stats, holes as columns. Rows: Score, Fairway Hit, Green in Regulation, Putts, Chip Shots, Greenside Sand Shots, Penalties. Fairway cells show a check mark, a direction arrow or letter, or a cross; GIR cells show a check mark or a cross, sometimes with a miss direction. A dash or empty cell is `not_recorded` (or -1 for the number rows, with `stats_visible` true).
- **`benchmarks`** (Round Summary, then More Stats or Basic Stats, then Benchmarks). A table with rows GROSS, NET, FRONT 9, BACK 9, PAR OR BETTER, FAIRWAYS HIT, GREENS IN REGULATION, PUTTS PER HOLE, TOTAL PUTTS, HOLES WITH 3+ PUTTS, UP & DOWN, SAND SAVES, PENALTIES, and columns THIS ROUND, a personal average, COURSE AVG and YOUR RANK. Use **only** the THIS ROUND column: GROSS to `displayed.gross`, FRONT 9 to `front`, BACK 9 to `back`, FAIRWAYS HIT to `fairways_hit`, GREENS IN REGULATION to `gir`, TOTAL PUTTS to `total_putts`, PENALTIES to `penalties`. Ignore the other rows and columns.
- **`share_scorecard`** (the Share Scorecard graphic). One image with all hole scores and their symbols, the course, the tee yardage and often a rating/slope pair such as "73.8/130": copy that into `rating_text` as printed. No per-hole par.
- **`other`**: anything else (hole-by-hole GPS screens, feed posts, the rounds list). Describe it in `images` and do not take hole values from it.
